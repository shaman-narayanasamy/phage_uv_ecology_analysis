# Researcher-authorship remediation for ISME Communications

Status: mandatory compliance gate identified from the live Oxford author
guidelines on 2026-09-01. This is a workflow record, not manuscript prose and
not legal advice.

## Why this is required

The current local manuscript began as an agent-authored draft. ISME
Communications currently permits LLM assistance for language correction and
translation but prohibits LLM drafting of manuscripts from prompts. The journal
also requires disclosure when AI assisted with manuscript content, figures,
tables, software code, data processing, or translation.

The current draft may be used to locate verified evidence and expose decisions,
but it must not be lightly edited and presented as researcher-authored text.

Official policy source:
<https://academic.oup.com/ismecommun/pages/author-guidelines>

## Required remediation

- [ ] Researchers independently write the title, abstract, introduction,
  methods, results, discussion, legends, declarations, and cover letter from the
  canonical evidence and primary literature.
- [ ] Researchers verify every reported value against
  `manuscript/claim_evidence_registry.tsv` and the canonical source tables.
- [ ] Researchers decide whether each AI-assisted figure, table, script, and
  analysis artifact may be used under the current policy, independently inspect
  or regenerate it as necessary, and retain that decision in the audit trail.
- [ ] Researchers draft and approve an accurate AI-use disclosure covering
  language, code, analysis, figures, tables, references, and data processing.
- [ ] If intended use is not clearly within the journal's permitted categories,
  the corresponding author obtains written guidance from the editorial office
  before submission.
- [ ] The independently authored manuscript is reconnected to the same
  machine-checked claims, citations, figure allocations, and inference
  boundaries.
- [ ] The full pre-submission audit and visual inspection are rerun after the
  researcher-authored text replaces the scaffold.

## Materials researchers may use

- Canonical result tables and checksums.
- Claim and analysis registries.
- Primary literature and the Zotero-importable bibliography.
- Figure source tables and visual grammar.
- Upstream software and repository provenance.
- The current scaffold as a map of evidence and unresolved decisions, not as
  prose to disguise through superficial rewriting.

Completion requires named researcher confirmation. An agent cannot mark this
gate complete.
