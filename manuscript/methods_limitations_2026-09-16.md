# Limitations: integration draft

This text belongs in the manuscript's dedicated Limitations section. It is not
to be distributed as repeated caveats through Results. The existing community
comparison limitations in `community_results_2026-09-15.md` remain applicable.

The study contains one membrane per condition. Repeated observations resolve changes across cleaning cycles and sampling fractions, but treatment and membrane identity remain inseparable. Model-based associations therefore describe this experimental system. The limited number of cycles also constrains assessment of temporal dependence and long-term community trajectories.

The genome catalogue was recovered under the executed 75% completeness and 25% contamination eligibility thresholds. These settings do not define a uniformly high-quality MAG collection, and residual contamination can affect attribution of genes to populations. Broad biofilm annotation terms identify an operational candidate set, rather than experimentally demonstrated biofilm functions. The combined definition was agreed after examining earlier exploratory lists, and is not presented as a prospectively registered gene set. SOS-associated expression and population diversity are distinct observations; neither directly measures UV lesions or establishes UV resistance.

Gene quantification used unstranded alignment-overlap counts. These can count an alignment against multiple overlapping features and are not unique-fragment or molecule counts. Technical-run aggregation does not create biological replication. Several execution versions could not be recovered from the available archives; the reproducibility record distinguishes verified runtime versions from environment declarations and unresolved versions.

Concentration randomisation assesses the allocation of supported responses conditional on the observed eligible features. Matching and genomic-block aggregation address measured detectability and local linkage, but do not reproduce every source of coordinated regulation. Whole-contig blocks are not inferred operons. Finite randomisation depth also limits the resolution of empirical P values.

The cleaning-performance outcome combines published mean readouts and exact reported regrowth lags. Digitisation and the absence of original phenotype-level replicates prevent full propagation of measurement uncertainty. The composite and its equal component weights were developed for this proof of concept. Association models use six outcomes per fit, with one residual degree of freedom in the categorical-cycle specification. Candidate selection and model fitting use the same dataset; fitted-versus-observed agreement is not external validation.

Suppression scenarios hold the fitted relationship and other predictors fixed. They do not model compensatory growth, compositional redistribution, phage kinetics or interpopulation interactions, and large suppressions may extend beyond the observed predictor range. A predicted improvement in cleaning performance prioritises an experimental hypothesis rather than establishing the effect of removing a population.

The population-genomic analysis covers a previously prioritised subset rather than a community-representative sample. Coverage-qualified genomic models involve different MAGs from the biofilm candidate set, and evidence is not transferred between MAGs sharing a genus name. CRISPR-derived links provide candidate host associations, not demonstrated infection or therapeutic host range. Experimental development will require isolation of the candidate organisms, recovery or isolation of suitable phages, and direct tests of host susceptibility and biofilm response.
