# Manuscript structure and style benchmark

Author-selected reference: Martínez Arbas, Narayanasamy et al.,
*Roles of bacteriophages, plasmids and CRISPR immunity in microbial community
dynamics revealed using time-series integrated meta-omics*, Nature Microbiology
(2020), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-020-00794-8.
Retrieved via the KB publication record and Europe PMC full text, PMC7752763.
This is a structural and stylistic analysis of the coauthored paper, not a claim
that all of its prose was written by one author. The author's KB voice guidance
and his current manuscript edits remain authoritative.

## What the reference does well

The abstract proceeds from a specific biological uncertainty to the study
design, quantitative findings and a bounded implication. The introduction
narrows from ecological motivation to the missing temporal evidence. Results
establish the sampled system and reference collections before interpreting
community dynamics, network structure and a selected population. Methods mirror
that chain and separate assembly, representative selection, quantification,
annotation, modelling and network analysis.

Paragraphs introduce a question or analytical purpose, explain only the method
needed to understand the result, provide quantities and direct figure/table
references, and interpret the result locally. Terminology is defined when it
first becomes necessary. Technical settings, extended analyses and additional
taxa are placed in Methods or supplementary material.

The figure legends follow panel order and identify units, sample numbers,
encodings and statistical details. Figure numbering tracks the biological
argument rather than the order in which scripts were run. Community context
precedes networks and population detail. Cross-panel encodings are explicit.

## Proposed outline for this project, pending author-edit review

1. **Introduction:** membrane cleaning as the biological/engineering context;
   uncertainty about community composition and expressed functions; why the
   deposited multi-omics data support reanalysis. End with the questions tested.
2. **Study design and integrated reference:** two membranes, three cycles and
   two phases; sample-specific MG/MT coassembly; across-sample representatives;
   common-reference remapping. Establish the inference boundary once clearly.
3. **Community composition and longitudinal organisation:** author's Figure 1
   decision; MAG, phage and 16S context; distinguish descriptive turnover from
   supported abundance differences. Preserve negative comparisons.
4. **Community-wide transcriptional differentiation:** full-universe results,
   effect sizes, organism-level heterogeneity and recurrence. Describe cycle
   interactions with the correct contrast interpretation.
5. **Functional interpretation:** post-model pathway/category results,
   including modest SOS-associated transcription and the absence of a broad
   repair trajectory. Avoid organising the paper around an unsupported
   DNA-damage mechanism.
6. **Genomic variation and host-phage context:** concise, evidence-bounded
   context; retain supplementary placement unless these analyses explain a
   supported central result. Do not force a population case study or network
   mechanism to imitate the reference paper.
7. **Discussion:** strongest supported findings, relationship to prior work,
   plausible interpretations labelled as such, limits of the repeated
   two-membrane design, and the measurements needed to resolve causality.
8. **Methods:** follow the audited data flow, then models and validation;
   separate sample/run units and versioned source from unresolved provenance.

## Editorial and formatting protocol

- Start each Results paragraph with its scientific purpose or finding.
- Keep numerical evidence beside the associated claim and figure citation.
- Maintain stable taxon names, abbreviations, cycle/phase terms and colours.
- Use compact biological headings; move tool inventories and thresholds to
  Methods unless needed to interpret a panel.
- Legends must stand alone: panel order, denominator, sample unit, summary
  measure, statistical comparison and relevant caveat.
- Reuse the reference's clarity and progression, not its stronger longitudinal
  design or conclusions. Three cycles cannot support its 51-point analyses.
- Apply target-journal submission formatting only after verifying current
  author instructions. This benchmark does not establish journal compliance.
