# Submission claim audit

Status: completed for GitHub issue #26 on 2026-08-29.

## Frozen scientific story

The manuscript describes longitudinal differences between one control membrane
and one phage-UV membrane across two phases and three cleaning cycles. The
strongest supported result is broad, bidirectional, organism-resolved
transcriptional restructuring after adjustment for phase and cycle. Cleaning
cycle dominates the leading expression geometry. Functional support is narrow:
SOS genes are collectively higher-ranked but have a modest median effect, while
the other seven predefined repair and stress categories are unsupported for the
adjusted membrane coefficient. Population-genomic results provide
coverage-qualified descriptive context for five selected MAGs only.

## Binding inference boundaries

- Condition, membrane identity, position, and history are inseparable.
- The 12 observations are repeated measurements of two membranes, not 12
  biological replicates.
- Model P values describe consistency within this system; they do not establish
  a population-level treatment effect.
- SOS-associated transcription is not direct evidence of DNA damage.
- Subset-first expression analyses remain provenance-only and are prohibited
  from the manuscript and supplement.
- Population genomics cannot support treatment-induced mutation, adaptation, or
  community-wide variation claims.
- The delegated 16S analysis is a complementary assay of the same observations,
  not independent validation.
