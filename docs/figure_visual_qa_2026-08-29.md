# Figure visual QA

Status: completed for GitHub issue #27 on 2026-08-29.

## Allocation

- Main Figures 1-4: community and expression geometry; transcriptome response
  architecture; recurrent genes; descriptive population genomics.
- Figure 5: conditional insertion point for the collaborator-owned 16S result.
- Supplementary Figures S1-S5: model diagnostics, interaction landscapes,
  complete functional coefficients, MAG coherence, and full descriptive
  population-genomic matrices.
- Supplementary Figures S6-S7: MAG and vOTU taxonomic context.
- The two analysis-led predecessor layouts are provenance-only.

## Visual review

All 13 one-page vector PDFs were rendered at 150 dpi and inspected together.
The allocated figures have readable panel tags, axes, legends, and text; no
clipping, overlap, broken glyphs, or blank pages were observed. Main Figure 1
was recomposed at a shorter height with a smaller top row and wider ordination
panel, reducing the unused space that had dominated the experimental-design
panel while preserving the full-width aligned community profile.

The MAG and vOTU trees remain supplementary because they provide descriptive
catalogue context rather than a central longitudinal result. Both legends
state that the trees are taxonomy-derived and not sequence phylogenies. The
stacked-bar community display remains preferred over an area plot because the
six cycle-phase positions are discrete observations and interpolation would
imply unobserved continuity.

## Visual grammar

Control remains `#8FCB8A` and phage-UV remains `#7E57C2` throughout main and
supplementary figures. Reused taxa retain their established mappings. Grey is
reserved for explicit unclassified, collapsed, unavailable, or contextual
elements as defined in each legend.
