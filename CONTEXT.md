# Phage-UV ecology

## Language

**Cleaning-performance score**: The frozen composite of removal-pattern and TMP-regrowth-lag scores, with one value per membrane-cycle. Higher values represent better cleaning performance under the agreed definition.

**Original biofilm-up candidates**: The 14 MAGs carrying at least one combined3905 biofilm-associated gene with a positive treatment log-fold change and transcriptome-wide FDR below 0.05.

**Remaining MAGs**: The other 334 MAGs in the 348-MAG DNA catalogue. Membership does not establish absence of biofilm activity.
_Avoid_: Non-biofilm organisms.

**Biofilm-down-only MAGs**: MAGs carrying a significant negative average condition effect for at least one combined3905 gene, and no significant positive average condition effect.

**No significant main biofilm response**: MAGs with tested combined3905 genes but no significant average condition coefficient. A cycle interaction can still occur in this class.

**No tested biofilm genes**: MAGs absent from the tested combined3905 membership table. This is a separate evidence status from a non-significant test.

**Hypothetical abundance reduction**: A proportional decrease in normalized MAG DNA abundance, used to calculate a conditional change in cleaning-score units. It does not measure attached-biofilm removal.

**Earlier 16S cohort**: The U+B40 specimens from the earlier study, evaluated separately from the current experiment. Their provenance does not establish a current-experiment cycle 0.
